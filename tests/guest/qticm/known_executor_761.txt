# QTICM lines Executor's ICM is known to answer differently from the Quadra
# (run_qticm.sh --known). Both come from dithering, which Apple's QuickDraw
# does and Executor's CopyBits doesn't (ditherCopy is drawn as srcCopy):
#
# 'smc ' compresses 8-bit pixels: the ICM copies the 32-bit source to an
# 8-bit offscreen first, and the Quadra's copy is dithered (1662 bytes of
# smc against Executor's 347 from flat colour bands); its decompressions
# then decode other data.

decompress.smc_.32
decompress.smc_.32.half
compress.smc_.16
decompress.smc_.16
decompress.smc_.16.half
# Cinepak into a 16-bit world: the Quadra's pixels are dithered.

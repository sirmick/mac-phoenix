# The look: Executor's definitions and theme drawing draw System 7's look; the
# platinum theme comes later (docs/executor/MacOS8-9.md "One Toolbox").
draw32.*	platinum look not drawn yet
draw8.*	platinum look not drawn yet
frames.*	platinum window frames not drawn yet (Executor's definitions draw System 7's)

# Colours and styles
color.brush.48.*	brush 48 is invalid on 8.6 (-30560); Executor answers a colour
color.text.45.*	text colours 45-47 invalid on 8.6 (-30563); Executor answers
color.text.46.*	text colours 45-47 invalid on 8.6 (-30563); Executor answers
color.text.47.*	text colours 45-47 invalid on 8.6 (-30563); Executor answers
color.accent.data	accent colour table: ctSeed 0 on 8.6, Executor's differs
metric.ThumbStyle	8.6's default is 0 (single arrows/proportional off); Executor answers 1
metric.ArrowStyle	8.6's default is 0; Executor answers 1

# Regions: shapes Executor simplifies to rectangles, part codes it answers or not
winregion.*	window part regions: rounded corners, kinds 7 (utility side), parts 1 and 8 (-30593 on 8.6)
button.4.*	bevel/round button regions are shapes on 8.6, rectangles in Executor
button.5.*	bevel/round button regions are shapes on 8.6, rectangles in Executor
button.10.*	button kinds 10, 13-15 are paramErr on 8.6; Executor answers
button.13.*	button kinds 10, 13-15 are paramErr on 8.6; Executor answers
button.14.*	button kinds 10, 13-15 are paramErr on 8.6; Executor answers
button.15.*	button kinds 10, 13-15 are paramErr on 8.6; Executor answers
track.*.live40	live value: 8.6 maps the thumb position differently (22 vs 30)
track.2.thumb.rgn	slider thumb is a shape on 8.6, a rectangle in Executor
misc.tab.*	tab regions: 8.6's are the tab shape (width from the title), Executor's the whole rect
misc.menubg.*	menu background regions: shapes on 8.6
misc.growbox.*	GetThemeStandaloneGrowBoxBounds not implemented (output left alone)

# Regions: shapes Executor simplifies to rectangles, part codes it answers or not
winregion.*	window part regions: rounded corners, kinds 7 (utility side), parts 1 and 8 (-30593 on 8.6)
button.4.*	bevel/round button regions are shapes on 8.6, rectangles in Executor
button.5.*	bevel/round button regions are shapes on 8.6, rectangles in Executor

track.2.thumb.rgn	slider thumb is a shape on 8.6, a rectangle in Executor

misc.menubg.*	menu background regions: shapes on 8.6
misc.growbox.*	GetThemeStandaloneGrowBoxBounds not implemented (output left alone)

# The theme collection and the private calls
theme.GetTheme.count	8.6's theme has 17 items, Executor's 9
theme.item.*	theme items Executor lacks or answers differently (hcol, patn, sbar, sbth, smoo, smos, smsk, sndt, thme, varn; name)
private.IsThemeCreditDialogAvailable	true on 8.6
private.GetThemeVariantList.missing	8.6 leaves the output alone on error; Executor clears it
private.GetThemeSoundMask	8.6's mask is 0x0000000f (the theme's sounds)
private.GetDesktopPixPat.name	"Mac OS Default" on 8.6; Executor answers ""
private.GetDesktopPattern.name	"Mac OS Default" on 8.6; Executor answers ""
title.*.system	the system font (Charcoal) is TrueType scaled without hinting: glyph shapes differ by a pixel here and there
